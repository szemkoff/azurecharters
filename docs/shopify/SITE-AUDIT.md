# Azure Yacht Group – Site Effectiveness Audit

Date: 2026-11-07 · session `b7d9a3fa` · Scope: working copy H (`192895877163`, preview `?preview_theme_id=192895877163`) and live store data.
Question asked: how well will the site help people solve their yachting problems, and turn them into inquiries?
Method: review of the pages, menus and templates built in this project. This is a qualitative review. No traffic or conversion data was used, and no ranking outcome is promised.

Status key: DONE · WIP · TODO · USER (waiting on the user)

## Summary

The site is good at answering "what can I do and who can help me". It is weaker at "can I trust you, and how fast can I get this done". For big-ticket yachting decisions, the second question usually decides whether someone gets in touch. Until H is published, visitors only see the older version.

## Strengths

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Navigation | Each need has its own route: Buy (brokerage new and used only), Sell, Charters (charter types / destinations), New Construction (builds plus Project management, design, designers, naval architects, shipyards). Most specific needs are two clicks away. |
| New construction | The strongest part of the site. The three project management options (with diagrams), the six-stage timeline, the design pages and the shipyards (photos and map links) explain a confusing process and present Azure as the owner's representative, not just a seller. |
| Listings | Real specs, "Upon request" pricing where price_max ≤ 100, and a collapsible intro so listings show sooner on mobile. |
| Contact | Every page ends with an inquiry form plus the phone number (754) 444-6729. |
| Honesty rules | No invented specs or testimonials. Shipyards are labelled as examples. Photos are credited. |

## Gaps

| # | Gap | Impact | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | No proof: no testimonials, case studies, completed deals or delivered projects | High. Buyers want to see that someone like them was helped. | Real client quotes or project stories, with the client's permission. Must come from the user, never written for him. |
| G2 | Response speed is not visible | High. Charter dates, survey deadlines and new listings are time-sensitive. | WhatsApp button (decision pending) and a stated reply time. |
| G3 | Charters are inquiry-only, with no indicative prices | Medium-high. Some visitors leave for direct-booking platforms. | "From $X per day / week" on each charter yacht, using real rates only. |
| G4 | Few services for current owners | Medium. Owners bring problems such as maintenance, refit, crew and slow sales. | Expand Service & maintenance with real services Azure provides. |
| G5 | The best work is not live | High. | Publish H. DONE 7 Nov. |
| G6 | No measurement | Medium. We can't see which pages produce inquiries. | Inquiry/click tracking, Search Console, a test inquiry (Plan phase 7–8). |
